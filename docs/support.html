Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Drag two matching pieces together on the workbench to merge them into something better. Keep combining junk into parts, parts into inventions, and finish each villager's request to rebuild their workshop. No accounts, no sign-in — just tap and play.
Can I play offline?
Yes. Tinker Lab is fully playable without an internet connection. Your progress is saved on your device.
How do I reset my progress?
Delete and reinstall the app to start over. All progress is stored locally on your device, so a fresh install gives you a clean slate.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. Tinker Lab has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions.
Is the game suitable for kids?
Yes — it's a gentle, non-violent merge puzzle with no chat, no social features, and no purchases.
