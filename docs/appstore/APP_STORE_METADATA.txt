DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tinker Lab is a cozy merge puzzle about rebuilding a steampunk village one
clever invention at a time.

The town's workshops have fallen into disrepair, and the villagers need your
help. On your workbench, drag two matching pieces together to merge them into
something better: scrap becomes parts, parts become gadgets, and gadgets become
the inventions each villager is waiting for. Finish a villager's request and
watch their workshop come back to life.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Merge matching junk into 25 inventions across 5 themed workshops.
- Help five villagers - the Lamplighter, the Mayor, the Professor, the Baker,
  and the Postmaster - restore their corner of town.
- Fill out your Recipe Book and discover how every gadget is built.
- Play at your own pace. No timers, no energy meters, no pressure.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
- Relaxing, satisfying merge gameplay you can pick up anytime.
- No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts or sign-in.
- Fully playable offline. Your progress is saved on your device.
- Designed for iPhone and iPad.

Pull up a stool and start tinkering.
